# Privacy data map

| Data | Purpose | Access | Retention direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity/contact | account and essential notices | user, narrowly scoped support | account life + defined legal window |
| Tenant/role membership | authorization | security/admin | membership + audit window |
| Targets/background/preferences | personalization | user, product services | user-managed; minimize collection |
| Attempt answers/snapshots/scores | testing, review, analytics | user, scoped academic/support | published policy; preserve score integrity |
| Payments/orders | purchase and accounting | user, finance | statutory/contractual policy |
| Audit/security events | accountability and incident response | compliance/security/auditor | risk-based immutable window |
| Content authorship/reviews | academic governance | scoped staff | content lifecycle + audit window |

Facilities will support notice version, required acknowledgment, export, correction, deletion/account closure, and retention jobs. Deletion is policy-aware: legally or academically necessary records may be de-identified or retained with documented justification. Never collect sensitive data merely because it might become useful.
