# Product constitution

UniAdmissionPrep is an admission-preparation operating system, not a question dump. Its durable assets are verified university-pattern intelligence, governed original questions, explanations, and explainable performance data.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. The product never claims university affiliation without a real agreement.
2. Internal content is labelled **UniAdmissionPrep Original**, **University-focused Mock**, or **Pattern-based Practice**—never official, leaked, or exact.
3. A university is not a test pattern. Patterns vary by program, intake, evidence, and effective date and are versioned configuration.
4. Every production question has provenance, publishable rights, review, approval, and an immutable version.
5. Every scoring policy is versioned. Every started attempt captures an immutable snapshot.
6. Historical attempts remain reproducible; corrections create events and revisions rather than silent overwrites.
7. Preparation Readiness is an explainable internal preparation indicator, never an admission probability.
8. AI can draft or assist but cannot directly publish assessment content or be the final MVP essay scorer.
9. Authorization is deny-by-default and tenant-aware. Answer keys are high-sensitivity content.
10. External university facts remain demo configuration until evidence is entered and approved by authorized staff.

## Product boundary

Initial release: identity/access, evidence and patterns, curriculum, governed questions, mock composition, exam attempts, deterministic scoring/results, entitlements, operations, and audit. Preparation, adaptive practice, planning, tracking, readiness, B2B, and AI stay behind feature flags until separately delivered.
